National News

Uganda Launches New Five-Year Plan to Combat Human Trafficking

The Government of Uganda has stepped up its response to human trafficking with the launch of a new National Action Plan running through 2031, setting out a coordinated roadmap for prevention, victim protection, prosecution, and partnership across government agencies. Officials leading the rollout also flagged a troubling trend: traffickers are increasingly turning to digital platforms to identify, groom, and exploit vulnerable people, with children continuing to make up more than half of all reported cases nationally. The new plan places a stronger emphasis on public awareness and prevention as a result.

International & UN

UNICEF Uganda Launches New Country Programme (2026–2030) for Child Protection

UNICEF Uganda has opened a new chapter for the country's children with the launch of its Country Programme 2026–2030, approved by the UNICEF Executive Board. The programme sets out a stronger, more coordinated push toward a Uganda where every child can grow up healthy, educated, and protected, building on recent gains such as the training of Para-Social Workers within community Child Protection Committees.

National News

Kampala Childcare Incident Renews Calls for Stronger Early Childhood Safety Standards

A distressing incident at an early childhood development centre in the Kampala area in April 2026, confirmed by the Uganda Police Force, has prompted parents, caregivers, and policymakers across East Africa to take a fresh look at safety and safeguarding standards in pre-primary and early childhood settings. The case underscores why consistent safeguarding policies, staff vetting, and emergency protocols matter in every institution that cares for young children.
