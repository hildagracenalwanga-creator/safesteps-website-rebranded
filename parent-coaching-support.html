Why This Matters

Parenting Has Never Been More Complicated — or More Important

UNICEF and the World Health Organization emphasize that nurturing care and responsive, positive parenting are fundamental to a child's healthy brain development, learning, and emotional wellbeing. In Uganda, UNICEF's National Parenting Training Manual promotes positive parenting as a key strategy for preventing violence against children — yet families are also navigating rising adolescent pregnancy, growing digital exposure, and the everyday pressures of modern life with very little structured support.

Parent Coaching and Support exists to close that gap — walking alongside parents and caregivers with practical tools, not just theory, so every child can grow up in a home that is safe, warm, and steady.

Our Approach

Practical, Not Just Theoretical

We counter modern parenting challenges by equipping parents with practical, evidence-based skills — not lecture-style theory. Support is available as private one-on-one coaching, small support-group memberships, workbook-based self-study, and larger family conferences for churches and community groups, so families can engage in the way that suits them best.

Every caregiver can be a safe place for a child's heart to grow.
