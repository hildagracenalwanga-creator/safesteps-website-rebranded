About Us

Welcome to Safe Steps Training Services

Safe Steps Training Services is a Ugandan consultancy dedicated to strengthening children, families, organizations, schools, churches, NGOs, and businesses through integrated solutions in Child Safeguarding, Parenting Excellence, and Leadership Development..

Read Our Story Get in Touch

Our Story

What began as The Heart of a Child, a heartfelt initiative focused on raising awareness about child safety - has grown into a professional consultancy and mentorship platform. We are dedicated to equipping parents, teachers, caregivers, children, and institutions with practical tools to build and sustain safe, supportive environments where every child can thrive.

Rooted in values shaped by years of experience in social work, child safeguarding, and community engagement, we provide tailored training programs, interactive workshops, policy development, and mentorship support. Our work empowers individuals and organizations to recognize, prevent, and respond to child safety concerns with clarity, compassion, and confidence.

While our values are inspired by biblical principles, Safe Steps Training Services is a non-denominational consultancy that warmly welcomes and serves families and institutions from all faiths and cultural backgrounds. We believe that safeguarding children is a shared responsibility - one that calls for intentional action, unwavering integrity, and a heart for service.

"Dear children, let us not love with words or speech but with actions and in truth." – 1 John 3:18 (NIV)

Thank you for choosing to journey with us. Together, we can build stronger families, safer communities, and a world where every child is seen, valued, and protected, allowing them to grow into their full potential and God-given purpose.

With heartfelt commitment,
Hilda Grace Nalwanga
Founder and CEO, Safe Steps Training Services

Our Mission

To strengthen communities by delivering innovative safeguarding, leadership, and parenting solutions. We empower organizations and families to build safety, integrity, and lasting resilience.

Every caregiver can be a safe place for a child's heart to grow.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted consultancy for safeguarding, leadership excellence, and family development, creating generations of safe, ethical, and purpose-driven leaders.

Our Core Values


1. Integrity : We hold ourselves to the highest ethical and professional standards, ensuring transparency in safeguarding and governance.

2. Compassion : We serve families and communities with deep empathy, dignity, and a heart for protecting the vulnerable.

3. Excellence : We deliver world-class, practical solutions and constantly evolve our expertise to stay ahead of global standards.

4. Collaboration : We partner intentionally with organizations, parents, and schools to co-create sustainable, safer environments.

5. Innovation : We leverage digital learning and modern methodologies to make impactful training accessible anywhere, anytime.
