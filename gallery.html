Impact Stories

Stories From the Field

Composite reflections drawn from the kinds of situations our three core programmes are built to address.

Child Safeguarding

A Reporting Line That Finally Got Used

A community-based children's home had a child protection policy sitting in a drawer — written years earlier, never trained on, never tested. During a safeguarding audit, staff admitted they wouldn't have known who to tell if a child confided in them.

Within three months of training, staff had a clear reporting line, a trained focal point, and the confidence to use both — and used it, for the first time, within weeks of completing the programme.

Details composited and anonymized to protect the privacy of the children and staff involved.

Parent Coaching

From Reacting to Responding

A mother of three enrolled in private parent coaching after describing her home as "constant shouting matches" with her pre-teen son. Sessions focused on positive discipline — not permissiveness, but structure paired with warmth.

Six weeks in, she described the same son asking to help her cook dinner — something that hadn't happened in over a year. "I didn't change him," she said. "I changed how I showed up."

Details composited and anonymized to protect family privacy.

Leadership Development

The Meeting That Changed a Culture

A mid-sized NGO's leadership team joined the Executive Leadership Programme after a period of high staff turnover. The turning point wasn't a workshop slide — it was a single facilitated conversation where leaders, for the first time, heard direct feedback from junior staff.

A year later, the organization reports its lowest turnover in five years, and credits a small, deliberate shift: leaders who ask more questions than they answer.

Details composited and anonymized to protect organizational confidentiality.
