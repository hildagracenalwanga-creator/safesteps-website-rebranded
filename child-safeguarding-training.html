Why This Matters

The Case for Safeguarding, in Uganda's Own Numbers

Uganda's national Violence Against Children Survey found that three in four young adults experienced some form of violence during childhood — 59% of girls and 68% of boys reported physical violence, with high rates of sexual and emotional violence also recorded. Globally, the World Health Organization estimates that roughly 1 billion children aged 2–17 experience violence, abuse, or neglect every year, with consequences that follow them into adulthood.

Every institution that serves children — a school, a church, an NGO, a children's home, a sports programme — carries a responsibility to close that gap. Weak policies, untrained staff, and unclear reporting lines are not abstract risks; they are the exact conditions in which harm goes unnoticed. This programme exists to change that, one institution and one caregiver at a time.

The Child Safeguarding and Organizational Resilience Programme

Purpose: help organizations establish and maintain child safeguarding systems that comply with national legislation and international standards, while strengthening the culture around them — not just the paperwork.

Assess

Safeguarding Audits & Risk Assessments

An honest, structured look at your current systems — policies, recruitment, supervision, physical environments, and reporting culture — to identify exactly where risk is highest and what to fix first.

Build

Policy & Procedure Development

Child safeguarding policies, codes of conduct, safe recruitment systems, and incident reporting & response procedures, written to be actually used — not filed away.

Train

Staff Training & Annual Refreshers

Practical training for staff, teachers, volunteers, and board members on recognizing, preventing, and responding to safeguarding concerns, plus induction and annual refresher sessions to keep knowledge current.

Govern

Board Safeguarding Governance

Equipping boards and senior leadership with the oversight tools to ask the right questions, review incidents responsibly, and hold safeguarding as a standing governance priority rather than an afterthought.

Respond

Investigations & Case Management Support

Guidance and advisory support when a concern is raised — case management processes, emergency response planning, and referral pathways that protect the child at the centre of every decision.

Sustain

Digital Safeguarding & Quality Assurance

Extending safeguarding into online spaces, plus periodic quality assurance reviews so your systems keep pace as your organization — and the risks around it — evolve.

Expected Outcomes

What Changes When Safeguarding Is Done Well

Institutions that complete this programme move toward full compliance with safeguarding standards, measurably reduced safeguarding risks, a stronger internal reporting culture, and increased staff confidence in recognizing and escalating concerns. For NGOs and schools working with donors and regulators, it also means improved donor confidence — and, most importantly, better protection outcomes for the children in their care.
